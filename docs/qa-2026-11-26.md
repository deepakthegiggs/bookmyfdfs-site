# QA audit: bookmyfdfs.in V2, 26 Nov 2026

**Scope.** I crawled the live site and compared it with a local build of `site-src` at b227041.
- **Live crawl.** I used plain HTTP with JS off, sent requests one at a time about 1 s apart, and covered all 20 sitemap URLs plus the feeds and assets.
- **Local build.** I ran `python3 gen/fdfs_site.py --state <copy of data/build-state.json>`. It changed 0 pages, and every live file is byte-identical to the local build.
- **Tools.**
  - Lighthouse 13.5 (from the npx cache) for the 390 px mobile lab runs.
  - axe-core through puppeteer-core in headless Chrome, for keyboard, reduced-motion and both-theme checks.
  - No third-party services.

**Owners.**
- p2 is `claude/site-p2`: card pages, Best, offer and spotted content in `gen/fdfs_site.py`.
- p3 is `claude/site-p3`: `gen/home.js`, home UI and `gen/site.css`.
- qa is this branch.
- Codex owns data and infra.

## Findings, by severity

### F1 · High · honesty: an events offer reads as a movie offer
- **Page:** `/offer/hsbc-allcards-bms-events-10pct/`. It is also counted on `/`, `/bank/hsbc/` and `/this-week/`.
- **Issue:** The terms say this is a live-events offer and "not valid on Movies and Streams". The receipt row does say "live events, not movies". But these all read as a movie-ticket offer:
  - the title and h1: "HSBC credit cards on BookMyShow: 10% off";
  - the meta description;
  - the JSON-LD `category: "Movie ticket offer"`.
- **Evidence:** `offers.json` has `category: "events"` and `excludes: ["Movies and Streams"]`. The Offer JSON-LD has `"category":"Movie ticket offer"`.
- **Fix:**
  - Put the category into the title, h1, meta description and JSON-LD name, e.g. "HSBC credit cards on BookMyShow live events: 10% off".
  - Set the JSON-LD `category` from `o.category`, e.g. "Live event ticket offer".
- **Owner:** p2 (`offer_pages` / Offer JSON-LD in `gen/fdfs_site.py`).
- **Test:** `tests/test_qa_crawl.py::test_non_movie_offers_say_so_in_title_and_markup`. The offer is allowlisted in `KNOWN_UNLABELLED_NON_MOVIE`; remove that entry once fixed.

### F2 · High · accessibility: My cards loses keyboard focus
- **Page:** `/`, the My cards panel.
- **Issue:** Every activation re-renders the panel with `innerHTML`, so focus drops to `<body>`. This happens on "+ Add my cards", on a bank chip and on a card checkbox. Escape doesn't close the panel. A keyboard or screen-reader user loses their place after each pick (WCAG 2.4.3 and 2.1.1).
- **Evidence:** I focused `[data-wopen]` and pressed Enter in puppeteer at 390 px. `activeElement` was BODY after opening, after the HSBC chip, and after Space on a card. After Escape, `#mc.hidden` was still false.
- **Fix:**
  - Before re-rendering, remember the focused control's `data-*` key and focus its replacement afterwards.
  - Make Escape inside `#mc` close the panel and return focus to `[data-wopen]`.
- **Owner:** p3 (`gen/home.js`).

### F3 · Medium · budget: /spotted/ is over the 20 KB cap
- **Page:** `/spotted/`, with the real export (38 leads).
- **Issue:** The page is 20,709 B gzip with CSS (HTML 11,759 + CSS 8,950), which is 229 B over the 20,480 B cap. I measured with Python gzip -9; other compressors give about 20.2–20.5 KB. `test_generator.Budget` builds fixtures, so it never sees this.
- **Cheapest fix:** drop the spotted-page `ItemList` JSON-LD. That takes the page to 19,903 B (−806 B). It also stops publishing unverified third-party claims ("Buy 1 get 1 up to ₹500…") as structured data.
- **Rejected alternative:** replacing the 38 inline source-link SVGs with one CSS `::after` icon saves only 304 B (20,405 B), leaving 75 B of headroom.
- **Owner:** p2 (`page_spotted`).
- **Test:** `tests/test_qa_crawl.py::test_real_data_page_weight`. `/spotted/` is allowlisted at 21 KB in `KNOWN_OVER_20KB`; remove that entry once fixed.

### F4 · Medium · infra: www has the wrong TLS certificate
- **Page:** `https://www.bookmyfdfs.in/`.
- **Issue:** It serves the `*.github.io` certificate, so browsers show a TLS error. `http://www` redirects correctly.
- **Evidence:** curl reports "subjectAltName does not match host name www.bookmyfdfs.in". DNS is correct: `www` is a CNAME to the apex, which points at GitHub Pages.
- **Fix:** In the Pages settings, re-save the custom domain so GitHub provisions a certificate covering `www`, then keep "Enforce HTTPS" on.
- **Owner:** Codex or the owner.

### F5 · Medium · freshness: every check will soon be 48 hours old
- **Pages:** all offer pages, `/` and the hubs.
- **Issue:**
  - All 6 offers were checked on 25 Nov, 22:01–22:03 IST.
  - The daily refresh workflow is not enabled (gen/README).
  - Under decision 141, offers go `needs_recheck` 48 h after their check, which is about 27 Nov, 22:01 IST.
  - A V2 publish today without a fresh export ships checks that are about 1 day old.
- **Evidence:** offers.json `checked_at`, the page stamps and the sitemap `lastmod` (2026-11-25) all agree.
- **Fix:** run the guarded refresh before the publish, or enable `.github/workflows/refresh.yml`.
- **Owner:** Codex.

### F6 · Low–Medium · accessibility: accessible names don't match the visible labels
- **Page:** `/`, the day strip and the copy-code buttons.
- **Issue:** The visible text is "Thu 26 Nov 4 offers", but the accessible name is "Thursday 26 November, 4 offers". The visible text "CODE AXIS200 Copy" has the accessible name "Copy code AXIS200". Voice-control users may not be able to target these buttons (WCAG 2.5.3).
- **Evidence:** Lighthouse `label-content-name-mismatch`.
- **Fix:**
  - Drop the `aria-label` on the copy buttons; the visible text already names them.
  - On the day buttons, start the label with the visible words ("Thu 26 Nov, 4 offers").
- **Owner:** p3 (markup is in `gen/fdfs_site.py`, re-render in `home.js`).

### F7 · Low · performance: home paints late
Lighthouse at 390 px with mobile throttling:

| Page | Perf | LCP | CLS | TBT | FCP |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `/` | 96 | 2.4 s | 0 | 20 ms | 2.1 s |
| `/offer/hsbc-premier-bms-bogo/` | 99 | 2.0 s | 0 | 0 ms | 0.9 s |

- **What's slow:** the home LCP element is the answer paragraph, with 1.3 s of render delay. The causes are the render-blocking `site.css` (9.4 KB) and 130 KB of HTML (25.7 KB gzip).
- **Fix (after launch):** inline the hero-critical CSS on `/` only, and defer the rest.
- **Not ours:** the Cloudflare beacon adds 10 KB of legacy JS.
- **Owner:** p3.

### F8 · Low · content: "Best" title vs the page's own disclaimer
- **Page:** `/best-card-for-movie-tickets/`.
- **Issue:** The title and nav say "Best credit card…" / "Best card", but the body says "We don't rank cards as 'best'". The disclaimer is honest; the title leans on the search phrase.
- **Fix:** choose one consciously. One option is the h1 "Which credit card is best for movie tickets? It depends on the cap" (the question form is already used). Record the choice as a decision.
- **Owner:** p2 (Best by benefit type is in flight).

### F9 · Low · brand: share card and icons used the old palette (fixed on this branch)
- **Issue:** `brand/logo/og-brand.png` (the share card on 13 pages) and the favicon, `apple-touch-icon` and `app-icon-192/512` (the JSON-LD Organization logo) were still navy and marigold.
- **Fix (done):**
  - The share card is repainted: ivory page, ink text, lime ticket-stub fill, olive accents. It is 1200×630.
  - The icons now use the `tokens.css` logo tokens.
- **Follow-up:** `brand/tools/build_logos.py` still hardcodes the old palette, so re-running it would revert the icons. Update its constants when the brand owner next touches it.
- **Owner:** qa (done). The `build_logos.py` follow-up belongs to the brand owner.

### F10 · Info: things noticed and left alone
- **JS-off day strip.** The static HTML day strip starts at the build day (Wed 25 Nov, disabled), and JS rewrites it to today. JS-off visitors see a past day first. Owner: p3.
- **Duplicate index URL.** `/index.html` returns 200 as a duplicate of `/`. The canonical covers it; GitHub Pages can't redirect it.
- **"Save Rs 100" quote.** "Save Rs 100 OFF" appears on `/` and `/spotted/`, but only inside an attributed, verbatim source quote (a blockquote). That's acceptable, and the copy sweep skips quotes.
- **Asset caching.** `cache-control: max-age=600` applies to the fonts and CSS (a GitHub Pages limit). The CSS is versioned, so this is harmless.

## What passed
- **Status and head tags.** All 20 sitemap URLs return 200. Titles and descriptions are unique, each page has a self-referencing canonical and exactly one h1, and the 404 page is noindex.
- **Links.** No broken internal links, including assets, the feeds and the JSON-LD logo. Every external link has `target=_blank` and `rel` containing `noopener`.
- **Sitemap.** It equals the indexable pages exactly. `lastmod` is 2026-11-25 everywhere: the V2 redesign changed no facts, and 0 pages changed against the published state.
- **JSON-LD.**
  - Every block parses.
  - Types are limited to Organization, WebSite, WebPage, BreadcrumbList and ItemList, plus Dataset/DataDownload on `/how-we-check/`, Article on `/this-week/`, and Offer only on `/offer/`.
  - No FAQPage, Product or AggregateRating.
  - Each Offer's name, description, cap, validFrom and validThrough match both `offers.json` and the visible text.
- **Stamps.** Every visible "Checked/Updated" stamp equals its WebPage `dateModified`. Offer stamps equal `offers.json` `checked_at`.
- **Copy.** There are no prelaunch terms in the HTML, JSON, XML or TXT files. There are no banned words outside verbatim quotes. "Verified" only appears as "not yet verified" / "unverified", or in the `/how-we-check/` definition.
- **Accessibility.**
  - axe found 0 WCAG 2.2 AA violations on `/`, an offer page and `/spotted/`, in both light and dark themes.
  - The focus ring (3 px ink outline plus a 7 px lime halo) is visible on all 40 tab stops tested on `/`.
  - The Filters dialog keeps focus inside and returns it to the Filters button on Escape.
  - With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, nothing animates or transitions.
- **Page weight.** Every page except `/spotted/` fits its cap on real data. `/` is 25.1 KB against its 26 KB real-data cap.
